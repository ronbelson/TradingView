No who-above-whom. No strength table.

## Fast

- TF mid (when present; on 4H this is EMA50 — still a fast)

## Slow

- SMA100
- SMA200 (skipped if TF slow is already SMA200)

## Arrows

- Fast cross over a slow → green ▲ on that slow
